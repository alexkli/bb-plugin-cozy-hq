Cozy HQ's desks, PCs, chairs, plants, bookshelves, sofas and a few wall pieces
are furniture sprites from [Pixel Agents](https://github.com/pixel-agents-hq/pixel-agents)
by Pablo De Lucca, used under the MIT License. The source PNGs and their license
are in [assets/pixel-agents](assets/pixel-agents); `scripts/embed-pixel-agents.py`
packs them into `office/pixelAgents.generated.ts`.

Everything else (characters, the cat, the lounge furniture, the room, wall art
and sounds) is drawn or synthesized by Cozy HQ's own code.
