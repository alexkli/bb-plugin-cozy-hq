# Cozy HQ: the hidden easter eggs

Spoilers ahead. This page lists every hidden thing in the office.

**How they work.** A hidden egg isn't outlined when you hold Alt (Option) and
has no label. It sits on a small clue in the scene, and hovering the clue
gives a hint. Most also happen on their own now and then, about once every
hour or two, and never twice within half an hour. Some only happen by day, at
night or in some weather. They need idle people in the office to play along.
Eggs that need a fountain, a creek or a front yard only show when the panel
has room for one.

## Every scenery

- **String lights:** click the very top edge of the wall, along the string
  lights, and they twinkle.
- **Easter eggs at Easter:** during the Easter festival, painted eggs are
  hidden around the floor. Click one to find it.

## 🏢 Classic office

- **Window washer:** click a window sill ("Something squeaks outside"). A
  washer comes down outside the glass on a cradle, soaps and squeegees it,
  waves, and a pigeon lands on his hard hat. On its own only by day.
- **Lost and found:** click the battered LOST + FOUND box on the lounge floor.
  It flies open and a few of its treasures come out with their owners' lines:
  a single sock, a rubber duck, a "World's okayest coder" trophy, a
  tupperware that hums, an umbrella, a mug and one roller skate.

## 🌃 Neon City

- **Reboot:** click the stuck pixel on the main street's curb. The city
  crashes in glitch bands, shows a NEON CITY OS boot screen with a progress
  bar, and comes back on from the top down.
- **The big cat:** click the can of cat food by the last bollard. A giant cat
  rises in the widest window, blinks, purrs and sinks away ("Nobody feed
  it.").
- **Fortune cookie:** click the fortune cookie on the first bollard. A drone
  flies the fortune to the nearest person, who reads it out.

## 🌲 Redwood Camp

- **The shy giant:** click the big footprint by the tallest redwood. A forest
  giant leans out from behind the trunk, waves and ducks back.
- **Slug race:** click the banana slug on the plank bridge. Two slugs race
  across to a chequered flag in about 20 seconds, with cheering and a photo
  finish. Only where the camp has its creek.
- **Fireflies:** click the firefly jar at a tree's foot. The fireflies spell a
  word over the clearing (LGTM, SHIP IT, NICE…), then scatter.

## 🏄 Surf Villa

- **Periscope:** click the red buoy in the surf. A periscope rises, looks
  around with a glint and dives.
- **The invisible sunbather:** click the lone flip-flop on the sand. Wet
  footprints come up from the sea, a towel spreads out, and a sun hat hovers
  over nobody.
- **Pool shark:** click the cardboard fin by the pool. A "shark" circles the
  pool while everyone shrieks, then lifts out: it's the pool robot.

## 🏙️ Sky Loft

- **DO NOT PUSH:** click the button left of the elevator. The tower creaks
  and everyone sways for a few seconds.
- **Visitors:** click the green light blinking in the city below. A saucer
  glides past the windows, beams up a rooftop antenna and zips off.
- **The commuter:** click the grey feather on the hallway floor. A pigeon in a
  tie strolls through the loft with its briefcase, and back.

## 🗄️ Dusty Annex

- **DO NOT OPEN:** click the taped-up box on the floor. Dust and moths, and a
  memo inside: "Please do not open this box."
- **The ghost clerk:** click the 1987 staff photo by the door. The clerk who
  never clocked out drifts along the corridor ("STILL ON LUNCH!") and fades
  through the far wall. "Did it just get cold in here?" On its own only after
  dark.
- **The snack machine:** click its dented side. A kick, and out drop snacks
  best before 1998.

## 🏫 School

- **Peanut the hamster:** click the hamster cage. Peanut escapes, zigzags down
  the corridor and comes back on his own; the wheel stays empty while he's
  out.
- **Paper airplane:** click the paper plane on a classroom floor. It loops
  over the school and lands on your desk as a note: "Can we have recess
  early?" …Fine.
- **Fire drill:** click the pull station on the back wall. Red lights, the
  alarm, everyone lines up while the principal times it. "Who pulled it?"
  "…Peanut?"

## 🏠 Startup House

- **The term sheet:** click the mailbox by the driveway (its flag is up).
  Everyone's sure the letter is the term sheet. It's a 2-for-1 pizza coupon.
- **Raccoon:** click the rattling trash cans by the side gate. A raccoon peeks
  out with the last slice and runs off up the side yard. On its own at night.
- **Ship-it gong:** click the dusty gong in the garage. The whole house cheers
  "SHIPPED!" with confetti. "…To staging."

## 🏔️ Mountain Cabin

The chairlift and the piste are out in the open: click the lift for a ride (or
a stop, a dog, rarely a yeti), the lift hut for its bell, and the piste (or
the summit flag) and two or three of the idle ones ride up together and ski
or snowboard down, sometimes over the kicker with a spin or a grab. They go
on their own now and then on a bright day; with nobody free, a stranger comes
down instead. Hidden:

- **Alphorn:** click the brass mouthpiece left on the sun deck. Someone carries
  the alphorn out and plays; the peaks echo the call twice, softer each time,
  and a far-off yodel answers. On its own on clear evenings.
- **Snowdrift:** click the lump in the snow with a red mitten. The drift
  wiggles ("MMMF!"), the ski patrol dog digs out a shivering ski tourer and
  shares the cocoa from the cask on its collar ("COCOA?! BEST. DOG."). On its
  own while it snows.

## 🌋 Villain's Lair

- **Workers of the lair:** click the padlocked suggestion box at the corner of
  the open floor. Henchmen picket outside your lair with NAPS!, PIZZA, DENTAL
  and NO LAVA signs until you give in to Pizza Friday.
- **Laser pointer:** click the laser pointer on the end of your desk. A red
  dot skitters round the lair and down the corridor, the white cat after it,
  and she lands on a henchman's head ("Not the laser again.").

## 🚀 Spaceship

- **Boarding party:** click the tiny unlabeled switch on the bridge. An airlock
  cycles open and three boarders march onto the bridge: a pirate admiral with
  a plasma cutlass and a parrot-drone, a cloaked fortune-teller and a young
  deckhand with a sputtering blade. The crew duck behind consoles, the duel
  goes back and forth across the bridge, and it ends when the admiral slips
  on a floating coffee cup. Then everyone bows and the boarders leave. On its
  own about once every couple of hours.
- **Self-destruct:** click the covered red button by the gravity switch. The
  viewscreen counts down from 10 while the bridge flashes and the crew panic.
  At zero: confetti, a cake and HAPPY BIRTHDAY. "Just kidding."
- **Nugget:** click the vent grate with the missing screw. Nugget the hamster
  rolls out in an exercise ball, careers up and down the corridor and rolls
  back home.

## ☕ Coffee Shop

- **Croissant thief:** click the half croissant on a terrace table. A pigeon
  swoops in, pecks, and flies off over the road with it. Another croissant
  shows up a little later.
- **DO NOT UNPLUG:** click the sticky note by the STAFF sign. The Wi-Fi drops:
  everyone holds their laptop over their head looking for a signal, and you
  suggest turning it off and on again.
- **Busker:** click the open accordion case by the fountain. A busker in a
  beret strolls up and plays a waltz while coins fly into the case. Only where
  the café has its piazza.

## 🏡 Home Office

- **Garden gnome:** click the little gnome in the corner of your garden. It
  hops round to face you for a moment, and you wonder if it just moved.
- **Manhole duck:** click the manhole cover in the first street. A rubber
  duck pops up out of it and sinks back down.

## For developers

Each scenery's eggs live in `office/themes/<scenery>/eggs.ts` (the classic
office's in `office/themes/classic/eggs.ts`, the Home Office's in
`office/themes/home/clicks.ts`). They're scenery clickables with
`secret: true`. In the preview, `__cozyHq.clickScenery("<id>")` with an id
from that file sets one off without hunting for it.
